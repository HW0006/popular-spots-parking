# Popular Spots Parking — 熱門地點停車追蹤

A bilingual (English + Traditional Chinese) parking guide for the South Bay spots people actually gather at: Santana Row, Westfield Valley Fair, San Pedro Square Market, Great Mall, Stanford Shopping Center, Los Gatos downtown, downtown Palo Alto (University Ave), and Stanford visitor parking. Prices, free hours, hours, Tuesday Popular Times, and Waze navigation links, plus one-tap shared crowd reports (Lots / Few / Full, 30-minute expiry).

中英雙語的南灣熱門地點停車指南：Santana Row、Westfield Valley Fair、San Pedro Square 美食廣場、Great Mall、Stanford 購物中心、Los Gatos 市中心、Palo Alto 市中心（University Ave）和 Stanford 訪客停車。價格、免費時數、營業時間、週二熱門時段和 Waze 導航連結，還有一鍵共享回報空位（很多／剩不多／滿了，30 分鐘後過期）。

Live site / 正式網站：https://hw0006.github.io/popular-spots-parking/

San Pedro Square links out to ParkSJ (https://parksj.org/) for real live garage counts in downtown San Jose. No live counts are invented anywhere else — everywhere else, "live status" is crowd reports from the last 30 minutes plus typical patterns.

San Pedro Square 可連到 ParkSJ 看聖荷西市中心車庫真正的即時滿位數字，其他地方沒有捏造即時數字，「即時狀態」是最近 30 分鐘大家的回報加上一般規律。
